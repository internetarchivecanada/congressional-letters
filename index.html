Letters from Congress

Letters that members of Congress published on their own websites, to agencies, companies and each other, preserved by the Wayback Machine. Each Congress has its own explorer; search here looks across all of them.

Search matches each letter’s subject, its recipient, its topics and the member’s name. Searching the words inside the letters comes once the text of every letter has been read.

Letters by Congress

Each letter is counted once, in the Congress in session on its date. Select a bar to open that Congress.

Each Congress

Every member of each Congress is listed in its explorer, including those whose sites held no letters.
